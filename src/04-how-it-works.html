How it works

Four steps.
One governed process.

Identify eligible data

Sherlock assesses your industry, records, data rights and commercial potential in a confidential initial review.

Together we agree which records can be used, what must stay private, permitted uses, approval requirements and commercial terms.

Approved records undergo controlled anonymization and validation before any release is considered.

Approved, protected data may be licensed under terms that define the buyer, permitted use, access limits and your share of licensing revenue.
