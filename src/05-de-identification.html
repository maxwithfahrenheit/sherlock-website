The Process

Data protection by design

Anonymization

Protect the information behind the work.

Our workflows are designed to identify and remove personal identifiers, confidential business information and other sensitive content according to the requirements of each engagement, while preserving the utility of eligible data.

Our Pipeline

A controlled, multi-stage pipeline.

Data is processed within a defined, access-controlled environment. Multiple stages of detection, transformation and validation are designed to reduce disclosure risk, with review tailored to the sensitivity and structure of the underlying records.

Governance

Your data. Your scope. Your approval.

Each engagement defines which records may be used, what must be removed, when data may be released and how it may be licensed. Only approved outputs are eligible for licensing. Our approach is designed around applicable privacy requirements, contractual obligations and enterprise-defined controls.
